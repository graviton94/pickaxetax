Taking a break is not waste. The structure is inefficient: every return re-processes the whole context from scratch (440,000 tokens on average). Keeping one session going for days makes that cost bigger.

Little was never used again. Most of it was used at some point, but until then it was re-read on every call. These are an oracle's numbers: the size of the opportunity, not the result of a policy one could run, and reuse is estimated from overlapping words. Without calibration, the most conservative range for fetching on demand is 16–28%.
