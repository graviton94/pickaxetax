지금까지 지어진 AI 연산 가운데 실제로 가치를 만드는 것은 얼마일까요. 이 숫자를 공개하는 곳은 없습니다. 그래서 그 지수를 만들고 있습니다. 이름은 연산 버블 지수입니다.

이 지수는 두 종류의 숫자를 한 장에 놓습니다. 위에서는 돈입니다. AI 인프라에 들어간 투자와 AI가 벌어들인 매출을, 1차 출처와 날짜를 붙여 비교합니다. 아래에서는 실측입니다. 하드웨어가 실제로 돌아가는지, 모델이 필요한 만큼만 계산하는지, 그 요청에 그 계산이 정말 필요했는지를 봅니다.

[Research note 9/10] How much of the compute we built actually creates value?

Of all the AI compute built so far, how much actually creates value? Nobody publishes that number. So we are building the index that will. It's called the Compute Bubble Index.

It puts two kinds of numbers on one page. From the top: money. AI infrastructure investment against AI revenue, each figure with its primary source and date. From the bottom: measurements. Is the hardware actually running? Does the model compute only as much as needed? Did the request need that computation at all?

The three gaps at the bottom multiply into one number, the effective compute ratio, and the money gap is shown separately. Forcing numbers with different units into one makes them easy to refute.

Only users can fill in the bottom half. So contributing takes one click and no sign-up: after an analysis in the web app, press "Contribute anonymously". You see exactly what will be sent first: structure and numbers, no text. Topic words are off by default and appear only when at least three independent contributions share them.

Anyone can add figures to the money side too, from public primary sources. They enter the index only after a second person has checked them against the original.

All the data is public, because even people who disagree should be able to recompute every number themselves.
